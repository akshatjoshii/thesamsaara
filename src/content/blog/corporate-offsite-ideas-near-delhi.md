Every HR team knows the challenge: find an offsite location that's easy to reach, fits the budget, and gives people something to talk about on Monday. For teams in Delhi NCR, **Rishikesh** ticks every box.

## Why Rishikesh works for offsites

- **Reachable by road.** About 240 km from Delhi, so a single bus or a few tempo travellers can move the whole team — no flights to book.
- **Built-in team building.** River rafting is the original team-building exercise. Everyone paddles, everyone gets wet, everyone laughs.
- **Variety.** Adventure for the thrill-seekers, pool and café time for those who want to relax, and space for workshops.
- **Value.** Riverside stays with meals included keep per-head costs predictable.

## Offsite activity ideas

1. **Rafting challenge** — split into raft teams for the morning run.
2. **Beach volleyball league** — quick, competitive and great for mixing departments.
3. **Bonfire town hall** — share wins and plans around the fire, then switch to music.
4. **Karaoke night** — a reliable icebreaker for new teams.
5. **Riverside strategy session** — take the whiteboard outdoors for a morning.
6. **Awards dinner** — celebrate the quarter with a relaxed dinner by the river.

## Sample 2-day plan

**Day 1:** Leave early from Delhi, arrive by early afternoon, lunch, a team session, beach games, bonfire and dinner.
**Day 2:** Breakfast, rafting, wrap-up session and lunch, then head home.

## Planning checklist

- Confirm headcount and rooming preferences
- Decide on the agenda: work sessions vs activities
- Share dietary requirements early
- Arrange transport and departure times
- Ask for one all-in quote with a group discount
