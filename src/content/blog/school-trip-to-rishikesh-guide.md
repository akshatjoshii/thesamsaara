Rishikesh is a favourite destination for school and college trips — and for good reason. It combines nature, culture and safe outdoor activity, all within a day's drive of Delhi. This guide is for teachers and organisers planning an **educational tour to Rishikesh**.

## What students can learn

- **Geography and ecology:** the Ganga, the Himalayan foothills and how rivers shape the land.
- **Culture:** Rishikesh's ashrams, temples and the evening Ganga aarti.
- **Life skills:** teamwork, independence and responsibility on a group trip.
- **Environment:** why keeping rivers clean matters, seen first-hand.

## Sample 3-day itinerary

**Day 1** — Travel from Delhi/NCR by bus. Arrive in Shivpuri, check in, lunch, orientation and team games. Evening bonfire and dinner.

**Day 2** — Breakfast. Morning activities (nature walk, beach games; rafting for older students where allowed). Afternoon visit to Rishikesh town — Laxman Jhula, Ram Jhula and the evening aarti. Return for dinner.

**Day 3** — Breakfast, reflection session or quiz, check out and return journey.

## Safety checklist for organisers

- Collect **parental consent** forms, including for any adventure activity.
- Share a **rooming list** with the property in advance.
- Keep a **medical information sheet** for every student.
- Agree on **activity rules** — rafting only with licensed operators and within age limits.
- Carry a **first-aid kit** and know the nearest hospital.
- Set a **buddy system** and fixed headcount times.
- Save the property's contact number with all staff.
