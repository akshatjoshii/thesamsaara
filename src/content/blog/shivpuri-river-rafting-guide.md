Shivpuri is the heart of **river rafting in Rishikesh**. Most of the popular stretches either start here or pass through, and the river beaches around Shivpuri are lined with camps. If rafting is the reason for your trip, this is the place to be.

## Popular rafting stretches

Rafting operators in Rishikesh run several standard stretches on the Ganga. Distances below are approximate and can vary by operator:

- **Brahmpuri to Rishikesh** (~9 km) — a gentle, short run. Good for first-timers and families with older children.
- **Shivpuri to Rishikesh** (~16 km) — the most popular stretch, with a good mix of rapids and calm floats. Around 2–3 hours on the water.
- **Marine Drive to Rishikesh** (~24–26 km) — longer, with more rapids. For people who want a fuller day.
- **Kaudiyala to Rishikesh** (~35+ km) — the longest and most challenging; usually for experienced rafters.

Rapids on these stretches range roughly from Grade II to Grade IV, depending on the section and water level. Your operator will brief you before you start.

## Best time for rafting

Rafting in Rishikesh generally runs from **around September/October to June**:

- **October–November:** clear water, pleasant weather. A great time to go.
- **December–February:** cold water and cool mornings, but quieter rivers and fewer crowds.
- **March–June:** warm days and high season. Book ahead on weekends.
- **Monsoon (roughly July–August):** rafting is usually suspended for safety as the river rises.

Exact opening and closing dates are set by the authorities each year, so check before you travel.

## What to wear and bring

- Quick-dry clothes (shorts or track pants and a synthetic T-shirt). Avoid cotton jeans.
- Sandals or floaters with a back strap — not flip-flops.
- Sunscreen and a strap for your glasses.
- A change of clothes and a towel for afterwards.
- Leave phones and valuables at the resort, or use a waterproof pouch.

## Safety tips

- Always raft with a **licensed operator** and certified guides.
- Wear your **life jacket and helmet** at all times on the water.
- Listen to the safety briefing and follow your guide's paddling commands.
- Tell your guide if you can't swim — you'll still be safe with a life jacket, but they should know.
- Age limits apply on most stretches; check before booking for children.
