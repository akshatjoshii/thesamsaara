Riverside camping is one of the best ways to experience Rishikesh. But the experience changes a lot through the year. Here's when to go — and what to expect in each season.

## The short answer

The best time for **camping in Rishikesh is October to April**, with **October–November** and **February–April** being the sweet spots. May and June are also busy and fun if you don't mind the heat. Avoid the peak monsoon months if you want river activities.

## Season by season

### October – November: the golden window
Post-monsoon, the hills are green, the air is clear and the river settles down for rafting. Days are warm, nights are cool — perfect bonfire weather. One of the most popular times for groups and corporate offsites.

### December – January: cosy and quiet
Nights get cold (sometimes close to single digits), so pack warm layers. In return, camps are quieter, the bonfire feels even better, and rafting is still on for the brave. Great for New Year getaways.

### February – April: spring on the river
Pleasant days, comfortable nights and full rafting season. This is a great time for school trips, birthdays and weekend escapes from Delhi.

### May – June: summer and high season
Hot afternoons, so the pool and river become the main attraction. It's peak season for rafting and family holidays; book early, especially on weekends.

### July – September: monsoon
Heavy rain, a fast and high river, and occasional landslides on mountain roads. Rafting and beach activities are usually suspended. If you visit, choose a cottage over a tent and keep plans flexible.
