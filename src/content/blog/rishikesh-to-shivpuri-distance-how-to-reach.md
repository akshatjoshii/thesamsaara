Shivpuri is a small stretch of river beaches and camps upstream of Rishikesh, best known as the hub for **Ganga river rafting**. It's close enough to Rishikesh for an easy day out, but far enough to feel like the mountains. Here's how to get there.

## Rishikesh to Shivpuri distance

Shivpuri is roughly **16–20 km from Rishikesh town** along the Rishikesh–Badrinath highway (NH-7). From the Laxman Jhula / Tapovan side, the drive takes about **35–45 minutes**. The road runs along the river and climbs gently through forest — keep your camera handy.

**Ways to get there from Rishikesh:**

- **Taxi or cab** — the most comfortable option, especially with luggage.
- **Shared jeeps** heading towards Byasi or Devprayag pass through Shivpuri; ask the driver to drop you.
- **Your own vehicle** — the road is good, but drive carefully on the bends and avoid driving after dark if you're not used to hill roads.

## Delhi to Shivpuri

Shivpuri is about **240 km from Delhi**, usually **6–7 hours** by road. The common route is:

**Delhi → Meerut → Muzaffarnagar → Roorkee/Haridwar → Rishikesh → Shivpuri**

Tips:

- Leave Delhi early (before 6 am) to avoid traffic at Meerut and Haridwar.
- Weekends and long weekends are busy — add an hour.
- Volvo and state buses run from Delhi's ISBT Kashmere Gate to Rishikesh; take a cab from there.

## Dehradun airport to Shivpuri

The nearest airport is **Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun**, roughly 35–40 km from Shivpuri. A taxi takes about **an hour to an hour and a quarter**, passing through Rishikesh.

## Haridwar to Shivpuri

Haridwar is about **40 km** away — roughly 75–90 minutes by road. Haridwar Junction has the most train options from Delhi and the rest of India, so many travellers arrive by train here and take a cab to Shivpuri.
