Rishikesh has everything a pre-wedding photographer loves: a turquoise river, white sand beaches, green hills and golden evening light. Here are some locations to consider.

## 1. River beaches around Shivpuri

The sandy beaches upstream of Rishikesh are the classic backdrop — wide open space, the Ganga flowing past and mountains behind. Shoot early morning or late afternoon for soft light. Our own river beach at [The Samsaara](/destination-wedding/) is available for couples staying with us or booking a shoot.

## 2. Laxman Jhula and Ram Jhula

The iconic suspension bridges and their colourful surroundings give a very "Rishikesh" feel. Go early in the morning to avoid crowds.

## 3. The ghats

Triveni Ghat and the smaller ghats along the river are atmospheric, especially around the evening aarti. Be respectful — these are places of worship.

## 4. Forest roads and viewpoints

The roads above Shivpuri wind through forest with sweeping river views. Great for travel-style and car shots (always pull over safely).

## 5. Cottages and pool

For a cosier, candid look, cottages, verandas and pool areas give you a relaxed, lived-in setting — perfect for the "getting ready" style of photos.

## Timing tips

- **Best months:** October–April for clear skies and comfortable weather.
- **Golden hour:** the hour after sunrise and before sunset.
- **Outfits:** flowing fabrics look beautiful by the river; bring a change of footwear for sand.
- **Permissions:** some public spots may need permission for professional shoots; check locally.
