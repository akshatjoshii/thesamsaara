A destination wedding doesn't have to mean a destination-sized bill. Rishikesh — close to Delhi, set on the Ganga and full of natural beauty — is one of the best places in North India for an **intimate wedding of 70 to 100 guests**. Here's how to plan one on a budget.

## 1. Keep the guest list intimate

The biggest cost driver is headcount. A wedding of 70–100 people lets you book a single property for everyone, which saves on transport, coordination and venue hire. It also makes the celebration feel personal.

## 2. Choose a venue where guests can stay

Look for a venue that offers **stay, catering and décor together**. When guests stay on site:

- you avoid paying for a separate banquet hall,
- you don't need shuttles between hotel and venue,
- and functions can flow from morning haldi to evening sangeet without logistics headaches.

At [The Samsaara](/destination-wedding/), cottages with 4–5 beds each can be booked per bed, which keeps per-guest costs low for cousins and friends.

## 3. Pick your season wisely

**October–November** and **February–April** have the best weather but are in demand. Weekdays and shoulder dates are often cheaper. Avoid the peak monsoon (roughly July–August) for an outdoor riverside wedding.

## 4. Let the setting do the work

With the Ganga, the river beach and the hills as your backdrop, you need far less décor. Focus your budget on a few key moments — the mandap, the sangeet stage and lighting — and let nature do the rest.

## 5. Plan simple, delicious food

Agree on a menu that guests will love rather than an enormous spread. Ask your venue about veg and non-veg options and include local favourites.

## 6. Build in guest experiences

Rafting, a pool afternoon or a bonfire night turn your wedding into a holiday for guests — at very little extra cost when organised at the venue.
