Packing for a camping trip to Rishikesh is easy once you know what the days and nights are like: active, sunny days on the river and cooler evenings around the bonfire. Use this checklist.

## Clothes

- **Quick-dry T-shirts and shorts/track pants** for rafting and beach games.
- **A warm layer** — fleece or hoodie (October–March, add a proper jacket).
- **Comfortable clothes for the evening** around the bonfire.
- **Swimwear** if you plan to use the pool.
- **A cap or hat** for the sun.

## Footwear

- **Sandals or floaters with a back strap** for rafting and the beach.
- **Trainers** for walks and travel.

## Essentials

- Sunscreen (SPF 30+) and lip balm
- Sunglasses with a strap
- A small torch or headlamp
- Reusable water bottle
- Insect repellent
- Personal medication and a basic first-aid kit
- ID proof (carry an original for check-in)
- A power bank
- A waterproof pouch for your phone

## For rafting

- A change of clothes and a small towel
- A bag for wet clothes
- Leave jewellery and valuables at the camp

## What to leave at home

- Heavy denim and cotton (it stays wet)
- Flip-flops for rafting (they won't stay on)
- Single-use plastics — help keep the Ganga clean
