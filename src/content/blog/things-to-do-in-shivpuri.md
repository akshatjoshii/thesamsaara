Most people come to Shivpuri for one thing: rafting. But stay a night or two and you'll find it's a lovely place to slow down. Here's what else to do.

## Relax on the river beach
Sit on the sand, dip your feet in the cold Ganga and watch rafts float past. Mornings are especially peaceful.

## Go camping
Spend a night in a [riverside tent](/riverside-camping/) — the sound of the river and a sky full of stars are the main attractions.

## Bonfire evenings
As the sun drops behind the hills, the bonfire comes alive with music, snacks and conversation.

## Swim, play and unwind
Cool off in the pool, play beach volleyball or settle into indoor games and karaoke with your group.

## Eat well
Try a woodfire pizza or a Samsaara burger at our [café](/cafe/), or go for local favourites like poori bhaji and peanut chaat.

## Short nature walks
The forested hills around Shivpuri are good for gentle walks and birdwatching. Ask locally for safe trails, and avoid walking alone after dark.

## Day trip into Rishikesh
Rishikesh town is under an hour away. Walk across Laxman Jhula, browse the cafés of Tapovan and watch the evening Ganga aarti at Triveni Ghat or Parmarth Niketan.

## Plan a celebration
Shivpuri makes a memorable setting for [birthdays](/birthday-celebrations/), [team outings](/corporate-offsite/) and [intimate weddings](/destination-wedding/).

Whatever you choose, stay right on the river at [The Samsaara](/) — from ₹2,000 per person with meals included.
